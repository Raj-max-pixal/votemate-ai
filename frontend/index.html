What is EVM?

Electronic Voting Machine — a tamper-proof device used to cast and record votes in India. It has two units: the Control Unit (with polling officer) and Ballot Unit (with voter). Each EVM can store 64,000 votes and works on batteries.

What is VVPAT?

Voter Verifiable Paper Audit Trail — prints a slip showing your voted candidate symbol for 7 seconds. Ensures transparency and allows you to verify your vote was recorded correctly before the slip drops into a sealed box.

What is MCC?

Model Code of Conduct — a set of guidelines by ECI that parties and candidates must follow from election announcement till results. Bans freebies, hate speech, misuse of government resources during election period.

Who is ECI?

Election Commission of India — the constitutional body responsible for administering all elections. Founded in 1950, it is headed by the Chief Election Commissioner and ensures free and fair elections across the country.
